# TheBetterPlan - AI-powered Urban Planner  

**The Better Plan** is an interactive urban planning tool that combines climate, population, and other socioeconomic data with **Generative AI** to provide actionable recommendations for sustainable cities.  

**Checkout the website :** [The Better Plan](http://thebetterplan.streamlit.app/)  
> **Note for Hackathon Demo:** Currently, the app uses **data of Japan only**. 

---

## Features
- **Climate Risk**: Temperature, precipitation, and runoff from ERA5.  
- **Urban Vulnerability**: Population density and electricity access (NASA SDG 7.1.1).  
- **AI Planner**: Gemini AI provides context-aware, location-specific interventions.  
- **Interactive Maps**: Click to assess urban heat, drought, flood, and energy poverty risks.  

---

## Data Sources
- ERA5 (Copernicus) – climate variables  
- WorldPop – population density  
- NASA SDG 7.1.1 – electricity access  

---

## How It Works
1. Load datasets and visualize as interactive heatmaps.  
2. Click a location → sample climate, population, electricity data.  
3. Risk detection: Urban Heat Island, Drought, Flood, Energy Poverty.  
4. AI generates actionable recommendations for city planners.
